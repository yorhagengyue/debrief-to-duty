Debrief to Duty · solution diagram

An intelligence pill for data hall cooling loss, captured from operators' own incident reviews, approved by a person, and applied by duty teams during the next incident.

①  Business problem. Know-how for the first hour of a cooling loss does not travel between sites, years or companies. Microsoft lost a hall to the same failure pattern in Texas (2018) and Sydney (2023); the 2023 Singapore cooling incident left 2.5 million payment and ATM transactions failed.
